# Product Requirements Document - east-spruce-design-build

**Author:** Joe
**Date:** 2026-02-10

## Success Criteria

### User Success

**Internal (editors — Joe, Elliot, future interns)**  
- Edits and publishes happen without a developer; interns can add portfolio (and later blog) content through a clear process.  
- Success feeling: "I updated it myself and it didn't take all day" / "I can trust an intern to add content."  
- Measurable: time to complete common tasks (e.g. copy change, new portfolio entry), frequency of in-house updates vs. relying on outside help.

**External (visitors)**  
- Visitors find services/portfolio, sign up for the newsletter, and take the next step (book 30‑min call, book low-cost consultation).  
- Success feeling: "I understood what they do and knew how to take the next step."  
- Measurable: newsletter signups, 30‑min call bookings, low-cost consultation bookings, contact form submissions.

### Business Success

- **3–6 months after launch:** More qualified leads, primarily visible as increased **phone consultation bookings** and **low-cost consultation bookings** (main early indicator that the site and funnel are working).  
- **Ongoing:** Strong **ad performance** (Google, Meta) and **newsletter performance** (opens, clicks, conversions); **overall increase in sales** and **good ROI** on the website and marketing effort.  
- Exact targets (e.g. "X consultations per month") to be set once current baselines are available.

### Technical Success

- Site is **reliable and performant** (e.g. fast load, minimal downtime) so visitors and conversions aren't lost to technical issues.  
- **Conversion and marketing tracking** work end-to-end (e.g. Google tags, analytics, ad and newsletter attribution) so the business can measure the KPIs above.  
- **Platform and content** are maintainable in-house (no ongoing developer dependency for routine updates).

### Measurable Outcomes

| Category | What to track | Notes |
|----------|----------------|--------|
| **Conversion (primary)** | Phone consultation bookings; low-cost consultation bookings | Main early success signal; baseline and targets TBD when current metrics are in hand. |
| **Conversion (supporting)** | Newsletter signups; contact form submissions | Funnel health and lead capture. |
| **Marketing performance** | Ad performance (Google, Meta); newsletter performance (opens, clicks, link clicks) | What's driving traffic and engagement. |
| **Internal efficiency** | In-house updates without developer; time or frequency of content updates | Whether the site is maintainable as intended. |
| **Business impact** | Sales trend; ROI on website + marketing effort | Compare pre- and post-launch. |

## Product Scope

### MVP - Minimum Viable Product

- **Platform and ownership:** New site on chosen website builder (Wix, Webflow, or Framer) with model-aligned design; owner- and marketing-editable without a developer.  
- **Main pages:** Home, Services, Portfolio (CMS-driven), Blog (CMS-driven), Contact.  
- **Funnel pages:** Ask the Expert, Educational Video, Project Planning Pack, plus 2–3 other funnel pages aligned with SIX + MAPS and AMI.  
- **Systems and tracking:** Connected intake (e.g. 30‑min call, low-cost consultation booking); conversion tracking (e.g. Google tags, analytics); newsletter signup and consultation/booking as primary conversion points.  
- **Content at launch:** Core and funnel pages go live; blog and portfolio CMS live with content added in parallel with campaigns (no need to preload everything before launch).  
- **MVP success:** Site live with main + funnel pages and conversion points; Joe and Elliot can update copy and add portfolio/blog content without a developer; consultation bookings and newsletter signups trackable; 3–6 month goal is increase in those bookings vs. baseline.

### Growth Features (Post-MVP)

- Additional funnel pages and offers as AMI/SIX + MAPS strategy evolves.  
- Expanded blog and portfolio content.  
- Refinement of messaging, offers, and conversion paths based on ad performance, newsletter performance, and sales results.

### Vision (Future)

- **Online store** for plan sets and drawings (passive, high-margin revenue) on the same platform.  
- Platform choice should keep this option viable.
