# Product Brief: east-spruce-design-build

<!-- Content will be appended sequentially through collaborative workflow steps -->

## Executive Summary

East Spruce Design Build is rebuilding its website to support a new marketing strategy built on Architecture Marketing Institute (AMI) guidance—structured funnels, educational content (newsletters and blogs), and low-cost consultation services. The current WordPress site is hard to maintain and confusing for the business owner and marketing director, and it feels incomplete to visitors. The goal is a modern, model-aligned site on a user-friendly platform that the team can update without a developer, with a clear path to an online store for plan sets and drawings. Success means a website that acts as the centerpiece of growth: clearer brand and messaging, easier ongoing updates, and a platform that can support future marketing and e‑commerce.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

The current East Spruce website runs on WordPress with a block-based editor that is difficult for the internal team to use. Neither the business owner (Elliot) nor the marketing director (Joe) are full-time website designers, so making changes is slow and frustrating—they spend a long time figuring out where to adjust things. Elliot has rarely updated the site himself; most past changes were done by the designer who built it. Integrating a new marketing push—AMI's Sunshine Island Express foundations, funnels, educational content, and consultation offerings—into the existing site feels overwhelming, slow, and clunky. At the same time, visitors see a site that feels incomplete and lacking in structure and content, which doesn't support the brand or the planned marketing system.

### Problem Impact

- **Internal:** The team can't iterate quickly on content or structure. Updates depend on templates (e.g. portfolio pages) or outside help, which blocks consistent marketing and limits control over messaging and positioning.
- **External:** Clients and prospects see an outdated, hard-to-navigate experience that doesn't reflect the quality of East Spruce's work or the clarity of the new marketing approach.
- **Strategic:** Without a better system, the business is missing opportunities for better clients and projects. The right moment to fix this is now, with dedicated marketing leadership in place and a clear growth ask from the owner.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

- **Staying on WordPress (or only redesigning on WordPress):** The core issue is the editor and day-to-day maintainability. A visual refresh alone doesn't solve confusion or slow iteration; the team has concluded that more user-friendly platforms exist for their needs.
- **Relying on a designer for every change:** Unsustainable for cost and speed, and it doesn't build in-house capability for content, blog, portfolio, and future store.
- **No clear path to growth:** The current setup doesn't support AMI-style funnels, educational content, consultation flows, or future e‑commerce in a way that the team can own and update.

### Proposed Solution

Rebuild the East Spruce website on a modern website builder (e.g. Wix, Webflow, or Framer) that offers an intuitive, non-technical editor. Use a model design the team likes (e.g. reference site + BMAD-style planning) and have a freelancer build the new site so it closely matches that vision. The platform must allow the business owner and marketing director to edit content, fix issues, and add CMS-driven content (portfolio projects, blog entries) without code or a developer. The site becomes the centerpiece for AMI-aligned marketing: clearer brand and messaging, educational and consultation flows, and a foundation for future paid and organic traffic—and later, an online store for plan sets and drawings on a platform that can support it.

### Key Differentiators

- **AMI-aligned marketing system:** The site isn't just an architecture portfolio; it's built to support a structured funnel, educational content, and low-cost consultation services so East Spruce stands out through strategy and clarity, not just aesthetics.
- **Modern look and UX:** A design that matches a chosen model and feels current and engaging, with clear navigation and hierarchy, so the site visually and experientially stands out to clients.
- **Owner- and marketing-controlled updates:** No ongoing dependency on a developer for routine changes; the team can maintain and evolve content and structure within the chosen platform.
- **Future-ready platform:** A builder that can accommodate an online store for plan sets and drawings, supporting passive income and integrated workflows as the business grows.

## Target Users

### Primary Users (Internal)

**Content owners and marketing agents** who maintain the site and run marketing—no full-time web designers.

- **Elliot (business owner)**  
  Context: Owner, sets brand direction; doesn't want to fight the site.  
  Good day: Refines copy and messaging on the site so it matches how he wants the brand to sound; otherwise stays hands-off.  
  Success: Can tweak text and key messages without getting lost in the editor or calling a developer.

- **Joe (marketing director)**  
  Context: Runs marketing, content, and performance; needs to move fast.  
  Good day: Updates page layouts, marketing copy, and links; adds or edits blog and portfolio content; adds conversion tracking (e.g. Google tags, analytics); checks performance of Google Ads and email newsletter.  
  Success: Owns updates and tracking in one place, with minimal technical friction.

- **Future interns (content contributors)**  
  Context: Add portfolio and blog content; need clear, safe process.  
  Good day: Create new portfolio entries by uploading photos, filling in structured fields (context, captions, etc.), and submitting for approval/publish via a defined workflow.  
  Success: Publish-ready portfolio (and later blog) content without touching code or guessing where things go.

### Secondary Users (External)

- **Service seekers (potential design clients)**  
  People looking for residential design/build services. Find East Spruce via referrals, Google Ads, Meta ads, organic search, or cold outreach.  
  Ideal path: Land on homepage → browse portfolio/services or read blog → sign up for newsletter → book 30‑min call → book low-cost in-person consultation or contact → commit to a higher-ticket service.  
  **Value to business:** Higher-quality pipeline, fewer bad-fit projects, better close rates, more predictable backlog.

- **Plan set buyers (future)**  
  People looking to buy plan sets and drawings. Expected to arrive mainly from paid ads and social.  
  **Value to business:** Passive, high-margin income from a more streamlined sales process.

## Success Metrics

### User Success

**Internal (editors)**  
- **Outcomes:** Edits and publishes happen without a developer; interns can add portfolio (and later blog) content through a clear process.  
- **"Worth it":** "I updated it myself and it didn't take all day" / "I can trust an intern to add content."  
- **Behaviors to track:** Time to complete common tasks (e.g. copy change, new portfolio entry), frequency of in-house updates vs. relying on outside help.

**External (visitors)**  
- **Outcomes:** Visitors find services/portfolio, sign up for the newsletter, and take the next step (book 30‑min call, book low-cost consultation).  
- **"Worth it":** "I understood what they do and knew how to take the next step."  
- **Behaviors to track:** Newsletter signups, 30‑min call bookings, low-cost consultation bookings, contact form submissions.

### Business Objectives

- **3–6 months after launch:** More qualified leads and sales, primarily visible as **increased phone consultation bookings** and **low-cost consultation bookings** (main early indicator that the site and funnel are working).  
- **Ongoing:** Strong **ad performance** (Google, Meta) and **newsletter performance** (opens, clicks, conversions from email); **overall increase in sales** and **good ROI** on the website and marketing effort.  
- **Note:** Exact targets (e.g. "X consultations per month") to be set once current baselines are available.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

**Platform and ownership**  
- New site built on chosen website builder (Wix, Webflow, or Framer) with a model-aligned design.  
- Owner- and marketing-editable: no developer needed for copy, links, or content updates.  
- Ability to implement **SIX + MAPS** strategies: refreshed website with recommended landing pages and connected systems for streamlined client intake.

**Main pages (MVP)**  
- **Home** — Entry point, value prop, clear path into funnel.  
- **Services** — What East Spruce offers.  
- **Portfolio** — Project showcase (CMS-driven so you can add/update projects).  
- **Blog** — Educational content (CMS-driven; content can be added in parallel with launch and campaigns).  
- **Contact** — Inquiry and conversion.

**Funnel pages (MVP)**  
- **Ask the Expert** (or equivalent).  
- **Educational Video** (landing/offer).  
- **Project Planning Pack** (lead magnet/offer).  
- **2–3 other funnel pages** (to be named; aligned with SIX + MAPS and AMI recommendations).

**Systems and tracking**  
- Connected systems for streamlined client intake (e.g. booking 30‑min call, low-cost consultation).  
- Conversion tracking (e.g. Google tags, analytics) so ad and newsletter performance can be measured.  
- Newsletter signup and consultation/booking flows as primary conversion points.

**Content strategy at launch**  
- Core pages and funnel go live based on the model website.  
- Blog and portfolio CMS are live at launch; content is added in parallel with ads and strategic outreach (no need to preload everything before launch).

**Page-level detail**  
- Exact structure, copy, and CTAs per page can be defined in a later PRD/UX step; this brief fixes the page set and funnel intent.

### Out of Scope for MVP

- **Online store for plan sets and drawings** — Deferred to a later phase; platform choice should keep this option viable.  
- **Heavy custom dev or complex integrations** beyond what the chosen builder and its integrations support for intake and tracking.

### MVP Success Criteria

- Site is live on the chosen builder with main + funnel pages and conversion points.  
- Joe and Elliot can update copy, add portfolio/blog entries, and manage content without a developer.  
- SIX + MAPS landing pages and client intake flows are in place and measurable.  
- Early success signal: consultation bookings (phone + low-cost) and newsletter signups are trackable; 3–6 month goal is an increase in those bookings vs. baseline.

### Future Vision

- **Post-MVP:** More funnel pages and offers as AMI/SIX + MAPS strategy evolves; expanded blog and portfolio content.  
- **Later phase:** Online store for plan sets and drawings (passive, high-margin revenue) on the same platform.  
- **Ongoing:** Refinement of messaging, offers, and conversion paths based on ad performance, newsletter performance, and sales results.
