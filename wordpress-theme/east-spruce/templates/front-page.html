Designer-Led From Day One

Unlike general contractors, we bring design thinking before a single wall goes up. Siting, light, proportion, and sustainability are decisions made at the start — not afterthoughts.

One Studio. Full Accountability.

You have one phone number and one team responsible for design and construction. No finger-pointing between your architect and your builder.

Sustainability Is the Default

We don’t offer a ‘green option.’ Every project starts with high-performance building science baked in. Lower energy bills and a smaller footprint aren’t extras — they’re the baseline.

Licensed Architects, Certified Builders

Elliot Nolter is a licensed architect in PA and NJ, a Certified Passive House Consultant (CPHC), and a Sustainable Homes Professional (SHP). Credentials that are specific and verifiable.

We Qualify the Fit First

We’ll tell you honestly if we’re the right team for your project. The paid consultation is designed to give you real value whether we work together or not.

Craft That Lasts a Century
